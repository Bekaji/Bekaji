## 0. Read this first — what "accurate & live" means here

TSAT asked for (1) an accurate, live market survey, (2) monthly lead/sales numbers from it, and (3) a final project costing. Being straight about method matters, because the numbers are only as good as their source:

- **What this document contains now — live desk research.** Every market figure below is pulled from current, named, third-party sources (NCC, DataReportal/Kepios, KPMG, NapoleonCat, Meta ad benchmarks), dated November 2026, and listed in Section 8. This gives a **defensible model**, not guesses.
- **What makes the numbers *truly* accurate — a commissioned primary field survey.** Desk data tells us the market *size and shape*; it cannot tell us TSAT-specific truths like **willingness to pay for the Sky Phone**, real awareness, or local close rates. Those require asking real people in Abuja and Kano. We **cannot invent that data** — so Section 3 scopes and prices a proper field survey for TSAT to commission, and it is included in the final costing.
- **The lead/sales model (Section 4) is a projection, not a promise.** It is built bottom-up from real ad-cost and conversion benchmarks, with every assumption stated. The primary survey and the first 60 days of live data will tighten it.

## 2. Market sizing — Abuja + Kano (TAM → SAM → SOM)

The Sky Phone is a **premium hybrid (terrestrial + Thuraya satellite) device** — a niche, high-value product, not a mass-market handset. Sizing is built conservatively from the live data above.

| Layer | Definition | Estimate | How it's derived |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **TAM** | Adult smartphone users in Abuja + Kano | **~3.5 million** | Kano metro 4.93M × ~55% adults × 80% smartphone ≈ 2.17M; Abuja FCT 3.6M × ~55% × 66% ≈ 1.3M |
| **SAM** | Premium / satellite-relevant buyers (executive, government/security, business, affluent early-adopters) who could afford & need the device | **~420,000** (~12% of TAM) | The three target segments, scoped realistically for a premium satellite price point |
| **SOM (Year 1)** | What TSAT can realistically capture in year 1, two cities, one brand, pilot marketing | **~1,000–1,400 units (0.25–0.33% of SAM)** | Reconciled with the bottom-up funnel in Section 4 — well within the SAM ceiling |

> The SOM is **not** the old plan's "50% of market" — that was a target share of a loosely defined base. A premium satellite phone realistically captures a *fraction of a percent* of the serviceable market in year one. The bottom-up funnel below is the defensible basis for the targets.

## 3. Recommended primary field survey (to make the numbers truly accurate)

Commission this **before locking final targets**. It converts the modelled assumptions into measured TSAT-specific data.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Measure real awareness, willingness-to-pay for the Sky Phone, price sensitivity, channel & language preferences, best contact times, trusted influencers, and segment sizes |
| **Method** | Mixed-mode: face-to-face intercepts in key districts (Wuse, Maitama, CBD in Abuja; Farm Centre, Sabon Gari, Kano city) + phone + online panel |
| **Sample** | **n = 1,000** (500 Abuja, 500 Kano), stratified by segment; ≈95% confidence, ±3.1% margin of error |
| **Timeline** | 3–4 weeks (fieldwork + analysis) |
| **Deliverables** | Clean dataset, segment sizing, willingness-to-pay curves, refined funnel assumptions (CPL, CTR, close rate) to replace the modelled ones below |
| **Indicative cost** | **$4,500 (₦6,975,000)** — field agents, incentives, data processing, report |

## 4. Monthly lead & sales model (Year 1, base case)

Built **bottom-up** from the live ad-cost benchmarks, with an AI-assistant + human-close funnel. The social manager and WhatsApp inbound add an organic uplift on top of paid leads.

### Unit economics & assumptions (all stated, all to be validated in the pilot)

| Assumption | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Meta CPM (Nigeria) | ₦2,500 / 1,000 impressions | Live benchmark ~₦2,300, rounded up for safety |
| Click-through rate | 1.0% | Meta Nigeria average |
| Click → lead (starts WhatsApp chat / enquiry) | 8% | Click-to-WhatsApp efficiency |
| **Cost per paid lead** | **≈ ₦3,125 (~$2.00)** | Derived from the three rows above |
| Organic uplift (social + OOH + referral) | +25% leads on top of paid | Social manager, billboards, word-of-mouth |
| Lead → qualified (AI qualifies) | 65% | AI filters genuine prospects |
| **Lead → sale (close)** | **5% → 7.5%** (ramps as brand & reps mature) | Premium considered purchase |
| Blended CAC | **≈ ₦36,500 (~$23.6) per sale** | Total media ÷ total sales |
